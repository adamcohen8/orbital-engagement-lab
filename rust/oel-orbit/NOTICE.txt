OEL Earth precision-force implementations

IERS Conventions (2010), Chapter 6: published Love numbers, astronomical
arguments, frequency terms and pole-tide conventions. Numeric frequency
tables transcribed from Orekit 13.1.7 resources.
Orekit copyright 2002-2025 CS GROUP; Apache License, Version 2.0.
https://www.orekit.org/
https://iers-conventions.obspm.fr/content/chapter6/icc6.pdf

Ocean-tide Doodson and normalized C/S conventions are compared with Orekit's
OceanTidesWave and FESCnmSnmReader. No external FES dataset is included.

Earth radiation follows Knocke, Ries and Tapley (1988), Earth Radiation
Pressure Effects on Satellites, with OEL's apparent-disk Lambertian geometry.
The stock Orekit surface-cap quadrature is a different comparison model.
